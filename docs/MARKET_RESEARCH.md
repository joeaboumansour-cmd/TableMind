# Restaurant CRM Market Research

**Date**: February 19, 2026

## 🏆 Top Restaurant Reservation Systems

### 1. **OpenTable** (Market Leader)
**Strengths:**
- Massive diner network
- Seamless booking experience
- Review system integration
- Marketing tools

**Weaknesses:**
- High fees ($1.50-$2.50 per cover)
- Limited customization
- Poor customer data ownership
- No WhatsApp integration

**Pricing:**
- Basic: $149/month + $1.50/cover
- Core: $249/month + $1.50/cover
- Pro: $449/month + $1.50/cover

---

### 2. **Resy** (Premium Focus)
**Strengths:**
- Beautiful UI/UX
- Strong brand recognition
- iOS app for restaurants
- Table management

**Weaknesses:**
- Expensive
- Limited customization
- No built-in CRM features
- Weak analytics

**Pricing:**
- $189-$899/month + fees

---

### 3. **Tock** (Experience-Focused)
**Strengths:**
- Prepaid reservations

- Limited CRM

**Pricing:**
- $199-$699/month

## 💡 Market Gaps & Opportunities

### 1. **WhatsApp Integration** 🌟🌟🌟
**Gap**: No major competitor offers native WhatsApp  
**Opportunity**: 
- 2+ billion WhatsApp users globally
- Higher open rates than SMS (98% vs 20%)
- Preferred communication in many countries
- Two-way conversation capability

**Value Proposition:**
- "Reach customers where they already are"
- "Higher engagement than email or SMS"
- "International customers prefer WhatsApp"

---

### 2. **Offline-First Mobile App** 🌟🌟🌟
**Gap**: Most systems require constant internet  
**Opportunity**:
- Restaurants often have poor WiFi
- Staff need access while moving around
- Critical operations shouldn't depend on connectivity

**Value Proposition:**
- "Works even when WiFi is down"
- "Staff can use anywhere in the restaurant"
- "Never lose a reservation due to connectivity"

---

### 3. **AI-Powered Customer Insights** 🌟🌟
**Gap**: Basic analytics, no predictive capabilities  
**Opportunity**:
- Predict no-shows before they happen
- Identify customers about to churn
- Optimize table turnover
- Personalized marketing recommendations

**Value Proposition:**
- "AI predicts no-shows with 85% accuracy"
- "Automatically identify VIP guests"
- "Smart recommendations to increase revenue"

---

### 4. **Affordable All-in-One Solution** 🌟🌟🌟
**Gap**: Either cheap but limited, or comprehensive but expensive  
**Opportunity**:
- $49-99/month sweet spot
- All features included
- No per-cover fees
- Transparent pricing

**Value Proposition:**
- "Everything you need for $99/month"
- "No hidden fees or per-cover charges"
- "Enterprise features at small business prices"

---

### 5. **Easy Setup & Migration** 🌟🌟
**Gap**: Most systems require lengthy setup and training  
**Opportunity**:
- 5-minute setup
- Import from existing systems
- Intuitive UI requiring minimal training
- Video tutorials and documentation

**Value Proposition:**
- "Up and running in 5 minutes"
- "Import from your current system"
- "Staff learns in one shift"

## 🎓 Key Learnings from Research

1. **WhatsApp is the killer feature** - Nobody has it, everyone wants it
2. **Price sensitivity is high** - $99/month is the sweet spot
3. **Ease of use trumps features** - Restaurants want simple, not complex
4. **Offline capability is underrated** - Major pain point not addressed
5. **Data ownership matters** - Restaurants hate losing customer data to marketplaces
